## Content Ideas

Pick any of these angles or mix them — be natural, don't make it feel like an ad:

### 1. "Workout Anywhere" Moment
Film yourself working out in an unexpected place (park, hotel room, living room, rooftop) and casually show the app guiding you. The vibe: "I literally train anywhere with this."

### 2. App Discovery / Reaction
Screen-record yourself opening the app for the first time, browsing through workouts, reacting to features. Genuine reactions work best — "wait, it has 500 exercises??"

### 3. Morning/Evening Routine
Show your fitness routine and naturally include Libo as part of it. Quick cuts: wake up → open app → pick workout → train → done.

### 4. Before/After Workout
Quick transition from "about to work out" to "just crushed it" — with the app visible as what guided the session.

### 5. AI Scanner Feature
Point your camera at gym equipment and show the AI recognizing it. This is a wow-factor feature — genuine surprise reactions do well.

### 6. Challenge / Trend Format
Use a trending audio or format and adapt it to fitness/working out with Libo. You know your audience best — if something is trending, ride it.
